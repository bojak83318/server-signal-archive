### 1. BACK ONLINE

Find the September 2025 DediRock update announcing IPv6 availability on select KVM and storage servers.

### 2. THE SECOND-REPLY CIPHER

Find the 2011 thread *Miniature Markdown Guide*.

What's the sixth letter of that reply's first word?

### 3. THE COUPON TIME CAPSULE

Find the 2011 launch offer with a plan called **Baby VPS**.

Take the first four letters of the recurring-discount coupon and write them backward.

What do you get?

### 4. THE SEVEN-DOLLAR DECISION

Find the April 2014 announcement where LET kept the $7 VPS offer cap after a community vote.

What's the first word of the thread title?

### 5. THE INTERPLANETARY SPEED TRAP

In the LET YABS thread, find the parody benchmark for an Antarctica IPv9 VPS.

On the Mars row, how many Kbits/sec faster was **receive** than **send**?

### 6. THE FLASH-SALE FRAGMENT

In the 2020 Black Friday *Year of Chaos* megathread, find the €35/month Austrian dedicated-server deal with two 4 TB drives.

Split its promo code at each hyphen.

What's the third chunk from the left?

### 7. THE RAFFLE MATH

In the first-half 2019 LET Provider Poll results, suppose all listed prizes are awarded, with one prize per winner and every winner drawn from the valid voters.

How many valid voters would receive no prize?

### 8. THE DINOSAUR'S DISK FORMAT

In the March 2026 green-dinosaur avatar announcement, a file-system acronym appears immediately before "dark ages."

What does that acronym spell backward?

### 9. THE FIVE-LETTER SERVER CODE

Find the 2011 Jacksonville offer with a $14/year OpenVZ plan and a $16/year Xen plan.

Take these five letters in order. What word do you get?

1. From the **OpenVZ** plan's 5 GB storage line, take the **first** letter of the word immediately before **Space**.
2. From that same line, take the **first** letter of the RAID designation.
3. From the $16/year plan heading, take the **second** letter of the virtualization type.
4. Back in the **OpenVZ** plan's 96 MB memory line, take the **second** letter of the abbreviation after **Dedicated**.
5. From that same memory line, take the **first** letter of the unit immediately after **96**.

### 10. THE FINAL UNLOCK

Put your answers to **#1, #4, and #9** together in that order.

Put a space between the words and add **™** at the end.

**Which DediRock phrase did you unlock?**
