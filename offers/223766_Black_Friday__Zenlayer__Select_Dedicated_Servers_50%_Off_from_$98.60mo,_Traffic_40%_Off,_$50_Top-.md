**On traffic**

Traffic is priced by region, which is why the US boxes come out cheapest with the same 3TB. At the Black Friday rate it's $4.20/TB in the US and Frankfurt, $10.08/TB in Asia Pacific and $20.16/TB in Dubai. Anything past your package is billed at $0.0042/GB, $0.0102/GB and $0.0204/GB respectively.

You can change the package size on the order page and every size gets the 40% off. Miami with 10TB is $128.00/mo. If 1TB is enough, Miami drops to $90.20/mo and Ho Chi Minh City to $101.08/mo. If you'd rather not count traffic at all, switch the billing to Flat Rate and pay for bandwidth with unmetered traffic, also 40% off.

![Data Transfer or Flat Rate billing](https://i.postimg.cc/mgJJWxdM/3-billing-options.png)  
The 50% off doesn't stack with our usual term discounts, so monthly and multi-month come out the same.
