# BIMI Mark Certificates (VMC & CMC) for Inbox Brand Visibility & Trust
**Link:** [Original Thread](https://lowendtalk.com/discussion/219905/bimi-mark-certificates-vmc-cmc-for-inbox-brand-visibility-trust)

BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification) is an email authentication standard that allows organizations to display their verified brand logo in supported email inboxes. It works alongside DMARC, SPF, and DKIM to improve email trust, reduce phishing, and strengthen brand recognition.

[VMC (Verified Mark Certificate)](https://certera.com/mark-certificates/digicert-verified-mark-certificate "VMC (Verified Mark Certificate)") and [CMC (Common Mark Certificate)](https://certera.com/mark-certificates/digicert-common-mark-certificate "CMC (Common Mark Certificate)") are digital certificates used with BIMI to verify ownership of a brand's logo.

> **A VMC requires a registered trademark** and offers the highest level of brand verification, while a **CMC is designed for organizations that do not have a registered trademark** but can prove legitimate use of their logo.

Features of DigiCert Mark Certificates:
---------------------------------------

* Displays verified brand logos in supported email clients.
* Improves email authenticity using BIMI with DMARC enforcement.
* VMC requires a registered trademark and provides stronger verification.
* CMC supports eligible brands without a registered trademark.
* Enhances customer trust and reduces the risk of email spoofing and phishing.

Why do you need BIMI and Mark Certificate?
------------------------------------------

* Builds brand credibility by showing a verified logo in recipients' inboxes.
* Increases customer confidence and can improve email open rates.
* Helps protect organizations and users from phishing and brand impersonation.
* Strengthens email security while improving brand visibility across supported email providers.
